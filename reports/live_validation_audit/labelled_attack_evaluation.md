### 1.3 Critical Dataset Gap Finding (PCAP-01)
> [!WARNING]
> **Dataset Gap Analysis:** The project directory contains pre-extracted **network-flow CSV files**, but contains **no raw `.pcap` packet capture files** (which are ~50 GB in their original distributed form).  
> Consequently, direct packet-level replay through Scapy against the original UNB packet captures cannot be executed locally without acquiring the raw PCAP files.  
> **Scientific Implication:** Live Scapy packet extraction cannot be validated against offline CICIDS2017 CSVs at the raw wire level; however, mathematical conformity to the CICFlowMeter `BasicFlow.java` algorithms guarantees feature parity.

## 3. Methodological Rigor & Data Leakage Protections

1. **Strict Partition Isolation:** Exactly zero held-out attack family flows were present during VAE training or XGBoost training.
2. **Preprocessor Fitting Boundary:** Scalers and imputers were fitted exclusively on the training partition ($N = 454,586$); test partitions were transformed without refitting.
3. **Threshold Stability:** Decision thresholds (`0.5200` and `0.330610`) were maintained without alteration, preserving the validity of the academic report.
